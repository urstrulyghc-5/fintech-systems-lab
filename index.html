What this simulation demonstrates

These are separate responsibilities within a payment-processing workflow. Real implementations vary by payment method, provider and financial institution.

Validation and duplicate prevention

Invalid amounts should be stopped early. Repeated client references are blocked here to demonstrate duplicate protection. Production systems need stronger, persistent idempotency controls.

Failures and uncertain outcomes

A decline is not the same as a timeout. When a timeout occurs, the final outcome may be unknown. A real system checks transaction status before attempting a retry.

Ledger and reconciliation

Payment activity must be recorded and compared with authoritative payment-provider or bank records. This prototype summarises its own simulated records; it does not reconcile against a real bank.
