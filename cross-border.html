EDUCATIONAL SIMULATION

How does money move across borders?

Follow an international payment from a sender in India to a recipient in the United States. Explore the exchange rate, fees, participating institutions and possible outcomes.

This is an illustrative simulation, not a live remittance service. The exchange rate, fees and processing outcomes are examples, not current market quotes or promises. Actual payment routes depend on the provider and corridor.

3. Follow the payment journey

4

Payment routing and settlement

The provider uses its relevant banking or payment arrangements. Intermediaries may be involved, depending on the route.

5

Recipient credit and confirmation

The receiving institution processes the payment and confirms the outcome.

4. Why the outcome matters

Accepted is not always settled. An instruction can be accepted while checks or processing are still underway.

A delay is not proof of failure. A timeout or missing confirmation can leave the outcome uncertain until the provider checks its records.

Reconciliation matters. Institutions compare transaction records to identify missing, duplicated or mismatched entries and resolve exceptions.

Real-world rules vary. The payment provider, countries, currencies, banking arrangements and compliance requirements affect the actual journey.
