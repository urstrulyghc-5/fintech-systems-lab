Scope and limitations

This educational model demonstrates application-level payment logic. Real payment systems also depend on regulated participants, network protocols, authentication, fraud controls, settlement arrangements, security operations and provider-specific rules. The simulation does not claim to reproduce any particular provider's production implementation.
